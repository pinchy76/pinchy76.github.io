Oracle Primavera P6 XER Cleaner

Clean Junk Data from Your XER Files

If you regularly import schedules into Primavera P6 you'll know that a lot of junk data can get pulled into your database. Junk data can slow down your database and clutter your interface. This tool cleans up unwanted tables (like POBS and RISKTYPES) and even lets you tag data from different sources.
