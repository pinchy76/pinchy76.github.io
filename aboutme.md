## About Me

### Work

I work as a Planner, Scheduler, Project Controls expert in the infrastructure sector.  Currently I work for one of the leading UK consultancies.

I have worked on some of the biggest and most interesting projects in the UK.  I'm now working on the new Hinkley Point C nuclear power station.  Previously I've worked on some of Britain's biggest rail projects (Crossrail, London Thameslink, and the West Coast Route Modernisation) as well as supporting projects for London Underground, National Grid and others.

My speciality is managing large schedules.  I use data management and analysis techniques to help build and manage the schedules required by the biggest projects.  I build and maintain dashboards and reports that help focus management attention on the most important issues.

I am interested in how project data can be managed and used efficiently and effectively.  Why are new tools and dashboards being built for every project?  I am also interested in the human side.  How does data drive behaviour?

### Life

I live in Somerset, England with my wife and two children.  I've always been slightly itinerant having lived and worked all over the UK and briefly in France.  I'm excited by the prospect of being able to work from anywhere and I hope the pandemic accelerates the possibility of doing so.

I spend my spare time indulging in various hobby projects.  I enjoy tinkering with raspberry pi computers, restoring old machines and electronics and making art (painting, pastels and prints).  I enjoy music and reading.  I enjoy sport but I'm not INTO sport so don't talk to me about it, I can't do sport talk.
